# Kuberik Adopters

If you use Kuberik in production or in a meaningful project, we would love to list you here. Adopters give the community a sense of who is investing in the project and help newcomers gauge maturity.

## How to add yourself

Open a pull request adding your organization to the table below. Please include:

- **Organization** - name as you would like it displayed
- **Use case** - one or two sentences on how you use Kuberik (e.g. "Multi-region rollouts for our SaaS product across 6 EKS clusters")
- **Since** - the year you adopted Kuberik
- **Link** - optional, a public reference like a blog post, conference talk, or your homepage

You can also fill in less detail if your legal team requires it - even just an organization name is welcome.
